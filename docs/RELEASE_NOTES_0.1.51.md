# Miowser 0.1.51 (code 52)

- WebView playback keeps the orientation lock, aspect ratio, and chosen playback speed when opening the next video from the side pane.
- Confirming a typed playback speed with the check button or keyboard Done closes the speed panel in both players.
- A tap on an empty part of the screen can hide the controls while paused. Tapping again brings them back.

Try a side-pane handoff with landscape locked, Zoom or Fill selected, and a speed such as 1.37×. Check a second handoff and close the player to verify orientation restoration. In each player, confirm a typed speed, pause, and tap blank areas near the center and around the controls.
