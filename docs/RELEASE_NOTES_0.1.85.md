# Miowser 0.1.85 (code 86)

- Updated Night to your numbered family assignments: **1 Primary** `#ABD2FA`,
  **2 Secondary** `#7692FF`, **3 Tertiary** `#1B2CC1`, and **4 Neutral** `#091540`.
  Component colors continue to derive readable light, dark, and AMOLED tones
  from those families. Delete/error colors remain separate.
- Redesigned the theme picker as a horizontal gallery of rounded cards, with
  a prominent selection outline and each source swatch in its own pill.
  All source colors remain visible; larger palettes wrap within their cards.
  The selected theme is brought into view when opening the picker.
- Updated the theme reference with the 1–4 family convention, Night's mapping,
  and the gallery's centralized styling tokens.

On-device testing is left to you: check Night in your preferred appearance,
swipe through the theme cards, and check large-font layouts and Amber's ten
swatches.
