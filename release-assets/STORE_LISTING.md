## Google Play

**Name:** Miowser

**Short description:** Browse sites, find videos, stream or save them, and organize your Library.

**Full description:**

Miowser is a browser built around watching and organizing videos you find on the web. Browse pages, detect available media, and open a video in the built-in player or save a download for later. Add video references to your Library, arrange them in folders, and continue playback from where you left off.

The player supports subtitles, playback controls, and a Library playlist. The browser includes bookmarks, speed dials, site settings, and private tabs. Optional backups can save your settings and Library references to a folder you choose; media files are not included in backups.

Miowser can also download APK files from websites and open the Android installation prompt **only when you choose to open a downloaded APK**. You control the sites and files you use. Downloads may remain in public storage after the app is uninstalled.

Some websites and media formats may not be compatible. Site availability and content depend on the websites you visit.
